How it works

Book online, track every step

  1. 1

    Choose your service and a date

    Pick the coating you want, see an estimated price, and choose an available date.

  2. 2

    Reserve with a down payment

    Pay the down payment online on a secure payment page to hold your slot. It is deducted from your final price.

  3. 3

    Get your reference code and QR

    A confirmation email arrives with your reference code and QR code. That is your ticket.

  4. 4

    Drop off, inspection, and tracking

    Show your QR at the shop. We inspect your bike and confirm the final price, then you follow its status until pickup.

The price shown when you book is an estimate. The final price is confirmed with you in the shop after we inspect your bike.
